Stack Master – Privacy Policy

Developer: AR App Studios

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Stack Master, developed by AR App Studios. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the Stack Master mobile game.

2. Information We Store

Stack Master stores game-related information, such as game progress, scores and preferences, locally on your device.

We do not operate our own servers to collect or store players' personal information. We do not require users to create accounts or provide personal information to play the game.

3. Advertising – Google AdMob

Stack Master uses Google AdMob to display banner, interstitial and rewarded advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process information such as:

  • Device identifiers and advertising IDs.
  • IP address and approximate location.
  • Advertising interactions and app activity.
  • Device information and diagnostic data.

This information may be used to provide, personalize, measure and improve advertisements, subject to applicable consent requirements and Google's policies.

For more information, read Google's Advertising Privacy Policy.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, Google's consent management tools are used to request consent before advertisements are served.

Users may access available privacy choices through the Privacy Choices option in the game.

5. Local Game Data

Game progress and preferences are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data may remove this information.

We do not provide cloud-based game synchronization.

6. Children's Privacy

Stack Master is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. Advertising and consent settings must comply with applicable age-related requirements and Google Play policies.

7. Third-Party Services

Stack Master uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service. Google's handling of information is governed by its own privacy policies.
